## 1. Introduction

Welcome to The Liberty Council. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your interactions within our Sovereign Operating System remain secure, transparent, and private. This Privacy Policy outlines how data is handled across our multi-tiered agentic architecture—ranging from the user interface up to core orchestration and governance.

---

## 2. Core Architectural Privacy Commitments

Our system is built on a Secure, Contained, and Observable Architecture. Unlike traditional applications, our operating model prioritizes data sovereignty:

- Zero-Data-Trail Interaction: The User Interface (Tier 4) is designed to support zero-data-trail interactions, ensuring that your voice and text inputs are handled securely without leaving unnecessary footprints.
- Contained Processing: Sessions are managed securely via our Front-End Bridge (Libby Agent) and synthesized centrally through the Orchestration Engine (Core Logic) without unauthorized external logging or third-party data sharing.

---

## 3. Information We Do Not Track or Store

In alignment with our sovereign operating principles:

- No Unauthorized Profiling: We do not track or store your personal browsing history or conversational context outside the secure parameters required for immediate session continuity.
- No Exploitative Data Monetization: Your data, interactions, and sovereign session states are never sold, traded, or used to train external commercial models without your explicit governance approval.

## 4. Information Handled During Your Session

To maintain session state and provide optimal responses across our agentic tiers (Governance, Orchestration, and Councils), we process:

- Secure Session Metadata: Temporary connection parameters managed by the front-end bridge (Libby Agent) to route your queries accurately.
- Synthesized Outputs: Internal interactions coordinated by the Master Synthesis Engine (GuRu) strictly for delivering coherent, unified, and secure deliverables during your active session.

## 5. Security Safeguards

We implement rigorous security measures governed by our Architect Agent and emotional/tone regulators to ensure:

- Strict encryption standards for data in transit and in session memory.
- Continuous safety monitoring to prevent unauthorized access or system vulnerabilities.

You maintain absolute sovereignty over your data:

- Session Termination: Ending your secure session immediately terminates active data retention within the interface layer.
- Inquiries: For any privacy-related questions or governance requests regarding your data interactions, you may reach out through your designated system administrative portal.
